jourNOW

Speak your journal; Whisper transcribes it and any month exports as a typeset PDF.

Problem

Journaling loses to friction. The thought arrives on a walk, at 7 a.m., in the middle of something else — and by the time there is a keyboard it is gone. Voice memos catch the moment but pile up as unsearchable audio nobody plays back.

Solution

jourNOW is a mobile-first PWA built around one button. Tap to record; the audio goes to Cloudflare R2 and a FastAPI background task hands it to OpenAI Whisper, so the entry is already text by the time you look at it. Entries group by day in the Journal tab, and any calendar month exports as a “Classic Journal” PDF typeset by WeasyPrint.

Tech Stack

FastAPI React 19 PostgreSQL Whisper PWA

Key Features

One-Tap Voice Capture

Tap the mic and a live waveform and timer run until you stop; the upload happens on its own

Transcribed While You Move On

Whisper runs as a background task after upload, walking each entry from pending to done without you waiting on it

The Day, Grouped

Every entry lands under its own day heading, recorded and typed side by side

A Month as a PDF

Export any calendar month as a typeset Classic Journal, and the app blocks the export if a transcription in that month failed
