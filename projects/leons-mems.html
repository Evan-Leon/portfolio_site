Family photo app for organizing and sharing memories.

Problem

Family photos end up scattered across phones and devices, making it hard to share and preserve memories together.

Solution

A private family photo app where members upload, organize, and share memories in one place — with albums, timelines, and easy sharing built in.

Tech Stack

Supabase React TypeScript

Key Features

Photo Upload & Organization

Upload photos individually or in bulk with automatic sorting

Family Sharing

Private family space where all members can contribute and view

Album Management

Create and organize themed albums for events and milestones

Memory Timeline

Chronological view of family photos and moments
