Voice Trainer

Curates LoRA training pairs for each AI persona — and casts the voice it speaks in.

Problem

Fine-tuning an AI persona takes hundreds of instruction/output pairs in that persona's actual voice, but the raw material — real conversations, drafts, transcripts — arrives as loose prose with no instructions attached. Hand-writing the instruction for every sample is the bottleneck, and a dataset nobody reviewed teaches the model the wrong voice.

Solution

Voice Trainer reverse-engineers the instruction behind each pasted sample with an LLM, then puts every generated pair in front of a human: approve it, or send it back with feedback that drives a targeted regeneration. Approved pairs export as Unsloth-ready ChatML JSONL, a per-persona health panel scores when a dataset is actually ready to train, and the Voice Booth auditions the voice the persona will speak in.

Tech Stack

FastAPI React 19 PostgreSQL Temporal PydanticAI LoRA

Key Features

Persona Registry with Health Scoring

Every AI persona in one grid, each scored on freshness, quality, coverage and volume

Human Review Queue

Every AI-written instruction is approved or sent back with feedback before it can train anything

Staleness Is Derived, Never Stored

Pairs generated against an older prompt version are flagged stale and requeued in one click

Voice Booth

An AIOS auditions reference voices against its own casting brief and binds the one it will speak in
