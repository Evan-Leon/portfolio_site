Mind Palace

A graph of history you walk rather than search, built on the method of loci.

Problem

Facts you look up are facts you forget. A search box over a pile of documents answers questions on demand but gives you no structure to hang anything on, so historical knowledge never compounds into something you actually retain.

Solution

Mind Palace models history as a Neo4j graph joined by a deliberately small, curated set of relations — causal, temporal, participant, spatial — then adds the memory techniques that turn browsing into retention: ordered routes pinned through the graph, a required elaboration note on every pin, and a due-for-review list. The same graph is served to an orchestrator agent over a semantic API rather than raw Cypher.

Tech Stack

FastAPI Neo4j React Three.js Knowledge Graph

Key Features

Chronological Timeline

Every entity placed by region and date across twelve millennia, so a fact always has the same place

Routes Through the Graph

Pin an ordered walk of entities; every pin demands your own elaboration note, and the app checks the steps connect

A Palace You Can Walk

Eras and regions render as 3D hallways whose doors open onto the entities inside them

Curated Relations

Only causal, temporal, participant and spatial edges surface; the rest of Wikidata stays in a details drawer
