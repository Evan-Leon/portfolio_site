Spead Read

Read any article up to 3× faster. RSVP speed reading with ORP highlighting, triggered by Alt+S or right-click.

View on GitHub

Problem

Traditional reading is slow — your eyes waste up to 80% of reading time on saccades (jumping between words) and regressions (re-reading lines), not on comprehension.

Solution

RSVP flashes words one at a time at your chosen speed, eliminating eye movement. ORP highlighting marks the exact character where your brain recognizes each word fastest, so every glance lands in the right place.

Tech Stack

TypeScript Chrome Extension RSVP ORP

Key Features

RSVP Engine

100–1000 WPM continuous speed slider — find your perfect pace

ORP Highlighting

Blue marker guides your eye to the optimal recognition point of each word

Flexible Activation

Alt+S hotkey, right-click context menu, or select text first to read a specific passage

Full Playback Controls

Play, pause, rewind, skip — plus a finish screen showing elapsed time and effective WPM

Spead Read — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

No data is collected.

Spead Read does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or browsing data.

What the extension does

The extension reads text from the current webpage only when you explicitly activate it (via keyboard shortcut or right-click menu), and only to display it in a local reading overlay on your screen. This happens entirely on your device.

Storage

Your preferences (default reading speed and ORP highlight setting) are saved locally in your browser using Chrome's built-in storage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server.

Network requests

The extension makes no network requests of any kind. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no third-party services.

Permissions

The permissions requested (activeTab, tabs, storage, contextMenus) are used solely to enable the reading functionality. A full explanation of each permission is available on the Chrome Web Store listing.
