Grace's Tree

A private family tree that turns each relative into an interview worth recording.

Problem

Family history lives in the heads of the people who are still here to tell it, and it leaves with them. Nobody knows what to ask, so the recording never happens — and what gets handed down is a shoebox of photos with no names on the back.

Solution

Grace's Tree holds the whole family as one browsable tree, every person a postcard of dates, places, work and their own story. Alongside it sits an Arborist workspace that turns a relative into an actual interview: a 300-question bank across ten categories, AI-generated questions aimed at that specific life, and recordings that come back transcribed and translated, attached to the person they belong to.

Tech Stack

Django 6 React 19 PostgreSQL React Flow Celery Whisper

Key Features

Generational Tree

Four generations on a pan-and-zoom canvas, split by side, with couples and parent links drawn between them

Person Postcards

Any leaf opens a card: bio, birthplace, occupation, languages, and whatever interview is attached to them

A 300-Question Bank

Ten categories from Birth & Origins to Time Capsule, colour-coded by depth and flagged where a question turns sensitive

AI-Targeted Questions

Generation reads the person's own profile plus your notes and drafts questions only they could answer
