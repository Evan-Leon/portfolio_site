## Weeks
- 2026-W35 — the drive-in theater: full roadmap, DTF falsifier, and nine phases shipped in one day.

## Themes
August was the drive-in theater month. A single intense day produced a
twelve-phase roadmap and then built almost all of it: a scroll-driven 3D
"drive-in" at `/theater/` where the camera drives past lit poster screens, each
a link to a project page, with per-screen video that plays only while active.
The work was disciplined by falsification and by tests — the DTF spike
overturned the intuitive cost model (decode count dominates, the CSS 3D
transform is nearly free), and three separate real defects were caught by the
conformance kit and the Playwright suite rather than by eye. The month also
generalized its own method into the reusable `portfolio-showcase` meta-prompt
for adding future projects to the lot.

## Direction changes
- The lot moved from a fixed eight screens to an open-ended, count-agnostic
  design — every EVOsystem app is meant to earn a screen.
- The Codex Part-3 review turned the roadmap's biggest gap (no path to
  production) into an explicit DT10 deploy phase with a `CUTOVER: GO/NO-GO`
  gate — carrying July's deferred `evanleon.com` cutover forward as a named,
  gated decision rather than an open flag.

## Rules landed
No rules landed in-repo (rules-index opt-out), but the month surfaced eleven
candidates for the shared EVOsystem stacks, still unswept — carried verbatim
with promote tags (full text in `archive/session-logs/weekly/2026-W35.md` and
the 2026-08-25 daily):
- universal ×6 — falsifier-baseline ordering; pre-registered-protocol deviation
  + margin; decision-row-matches-its-own-grep; adapter `observe` reads written
  state; DOM-fake must be a real `Node`; e2e spec importing the page's own module
  can't fail (specialises `EVO-UNI-061`). `promote → universal`
- react-frontend ×2 — adapter must re-apply held state at end of `load()`;
  breakpoint override belongs in inline custom properties with `!important`.
  promote → react-frontend
- tooling ×2 — a `perspective`/`translateZ` wrapper silently swallows child
  pointer events; a falsification `sed` that breaks the build (not the test)
  reads as a false success. `promote → tooling`
- one untagged — GSAP `fromTo` `immediateRender: false` for tweens not at
  position 0.
